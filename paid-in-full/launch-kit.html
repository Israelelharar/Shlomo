שם המוצרName

Paid in Full: The Freelancer's Pricing & Getting-Paid Playbook

משפט סיכוםSummary, מוצג ליד כפתור הקנייה

A printable 20-page workbook with the worksheets, word-for-word scripts and checklists to price your work properly, stop scope creep, and get every invoice paid on time.

תיאור המוצרDescription, עם עיצוב

You quoted low because you wanted the job. The client asked for "one small change" — then five more. The invoice was due three weeks ago, and you still feel awkward chasing it.

Paid in Full is a practical, printable playbook that fixes the money side of freelancing. It turns the three biggest income leaks — underpricing, scope creep and late payment — into simple systems you can set up in a week.

What's inside

  • Minimum Rate worksheet — calculate the hourly and day rate you actually need from your income goal, costs, taxes and real billable hours.
  • Project Quote Builder — turn hour estimates and risk into a fixed price you won't regret.
  • Good / Better / Best package builder — give clients options without cutting your rate.
  • One-page proposal template — the six sections that make "yes" easy.
  • 20+ word-for-word scripts — "that's too expensive," raising your rates, discovery calls, deposits, and saying no to scope creep politely.
  • Contract essentials checklist — 15 points to have in writing before you start.
  • Copy-paste payment terms and a "no questions" invoice checklist.
  • The 5-step late-payment email sequence — friendly to firm, from 3 days before the due date to final notice, plus a phone script.
  • 7-Day Action Plan, client & invoice tracker, and a 12-month income dashboard.

Bonus: the calculator spreadsheet

An Excel / Google Sheets file that calculates your minimum rate, builds project quotes, and tracks invoices — flagging which reminder to send for each overdue one.

Who it's for

Designers, developers, writers, marketers, photographers, consultants and virtual assistants — anyone who sells their time and skills and wants to be paid properly for it.

The details

  • 20-page PDF, A4, printable or fillable on a tablet
  • Bonus .xlsx spreadsheet (works in Excel and Google Sheets)
  • 35+ scripts, worksheets and templates
  • Instant download

General business guidance, not legal, tax or financial advice. Laws on contracts and late payment vary by country — check your local rules.
